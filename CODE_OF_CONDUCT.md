# Code of Conduct

MDx should be a place where people can examine ambitious ideas, disagree
clearly, and still feel safe contributing.

## Our Standard

Be respectful, specific, and generous with context. Critique ideas and code,
not people. Welcome contributors with different experience levels and
backgrounds. Do not harass, threaten, demean, or expose private information.

## Where It Applies

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, review threads, and
other spaces represented as part of the MDx project.

## Reporting

If behavior violates this standard, contact the project maintainers privately.
Do not open a public issue that repeats harassment or private information.
Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when
needed to protect the community.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
